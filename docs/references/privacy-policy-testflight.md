# Likeminded TestFlight Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

Likeminded is a voice-first social app for private profile creation, AI-assisted circle placement, communities, scheduled group meets, recaps, and one-to-one matching. This policy covers the iOS and macOS TestFlight MVP.

## Data Collected

- Sign in with Apple identifier, and email/name only when Apple provides them.
- Google account identifier, email, and name when a tester chooses Google Sign-In.
- An app-generated device identifier used to operate and secure the service.
- Voice interview transcript generated during onboarding, plus voice interview transcripts and voice audio sent during the AI interview.
- Audio and video streamed while a tester joins a LiveKit group meet. Likeminded does not record LiveKit video in this MVP.
- Profile signals inferred from onboarding, such as communication style, social energy, trust pattern, and personality traits.
- Profile details a tester chooses to add, including interests, gender, date of birth, city, and pincode, plus private profile signals and circle placement.
- Circle and community membership, events, meetup RSVPs and recaps, meeting participation, Soulmate preferences and selections, match selections, chat messages, reports, placement actions, and tester feedback.
- Basic technical metadata required to operate and secure the service, such as app version and request timing.

## How Data Is Used

- To authenticate testers and restore their account on iOS and macOS.
- To create and restore the tester's private profile, personalize and explain circle placement, and operate communities, meets, recaps, and matching.
- To provide circles, communities, scheduled meetings, optional mutual Soulmate matching, and chat.
- To deliver AI voice interviews, LiveKit rooms, chat, safety reporting, and tester support.
- To protect the backend, operate OpenAI Realtime voice sessions, and improve the TestFlight MVP using submitted feedback.

## AI And Media Processing

Voice audio and transcripts may be processed by OpenAI to support realtime conversation and profile extraction. LiveKit processes audio, video, and room metadata for scheduled group meets, including connection metadata when a tester joins a scheduled meeting. Google processes authentication data only if a tester chooses Google Sign-In. Apple or Google processes account information when the tester chooses that sign-in method. Provider credentials remain server-side or in the platform SDK and are not committed to the app repository.

## Sharing And Tracking

Likeminded does not sell personal data and does not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Likeminded shares only the profile, circle, community, meeting, match, and message information needed for the tester's chosen social features. Private profile signals, placement reasoning, and voice transcripts are not shown to other testers and are not published as a public profile. Community, meetup, recap, and chat content is shared only with the participants who need it for that feature. Service providers receive and process data only to operate the functions described above.

## Retention And Deletion

TestFlight data is retained while the MVP is being tested, or until the tester deletes the account or asks the operator to delete it, except where a longer period is required for security or legal obligations.

## Account Deletion

Settings includes an in-app Delete Account action at Settings → Privacy → Delete account. For Sign in with Apple accounts, the app requires fresh Apple authentication and revokes the Apple token before completing deletion. Deletion then removes the Likeminded account and app-stored profiles, placements, transcripts, feedback, memberships, RSVP and recap data, participation records, matches, Soulmate data, chat data, messages, and reports associated with the tester. The tester may also contact the operator through the TestFlight feedback channel if in-app deletion cannot be completed.

## Contact

Use TestFlight feedback or the operator contact listed in App Store Connect for privacy questions. TestFlight screenshot feedback and crash reports may also be sent to Apple under Apple's own privacy terms.
