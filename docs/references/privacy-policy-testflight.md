# Likeminded Privacy Policy

Likeminded uses voice conversation to build private profile signals and suggest a starter circle placement.

## Data Collected

- Sign in with Apple identifier, and email/name only when Apple provides them.
- Google account identifier, email, and name when a tester chooses Google Sign-In.
- Voice interview transcript generated during onboarding.
- Profile signals inferred from onboarding, such as communication style, social energy, trust pattern, and personality traits.
- Profile details a tester chooses to add, including interests, gender, date of birth, city, and pincode.
- Circle and community membership, meeting participation, Soulmate preferences and selections, messages, reports, placement actions, and tester feedback.
- Basic technical metadata needed to run the service, such as app version and request timing.

## How Data Is Used

- To create and restore the tester's private profile.
- To suggest and explain a circle placement.
- To provide circles, communities, scheduled meetings, optional mutual Soulmate matching, and chat.
- To improve the product using tester feedback.
- To protect the backend and operate OpenAI Realtime voice sessions.

## AI Processing

Voice sessions and transcripts may be processed by OpenAI services to support realtime conversation and profile extraction. The API keeps the OpenAI API key server-side; the app does not store it.

LiveKit processes audio, video, and connection metadata when a tester joins a scheduled meeting. Apple or Google processes account information when the tester chooses that sign-in method.

## Sharing

Likeminded shares only the profile, circle, community, meeting, match, and message information needed for the tester's chosen social features. Private profile signals and voice transcripts are not published as a public profile. Service providers process data only to operate the selected feature.

## Retention And Deletion

TestFlight data is retained while the beta is being operated and until the tester deletes the account or asks the operator to delete it, except where a longer period is required for security or legal obligations.

## Account Deletion

A signed-in tester can use Settings → Privacy → Delete account. Deletion revokes the tester's Sign in with Apple authorization when applicable, then removes the Likeminded account and app-stored profiles, placements, transcripts, memberships, participation records, Soulmate data, messages, reports, and feedback. The tester may also contact the operator through the TestFlight feedback channel if in-app deletion cannot be completed.

## Contact

Use the TestFlight feedback channel or the operator contact provided in App Store Connect for privacy or deletion requests.
