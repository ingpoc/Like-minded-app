# Product Direction

Likeminded exists to help people who should meet actually meet, talk, and build relationships. It is not a generic social feed, dating app, community directory, or mental-health reflection app. The product's job is to use AI as a social operator: understand people through conversation, place them into the right human context, and help the first real interaction happen.

## North Star

The core product loop is:

1. AI voice conversation builds the user's living personality profile.
2. AI creates and suggests personality-fit circles.
3. AI suggests interest-led communities.
4. AI encourages interaction through in-app hosted meetings.
5. AI learns from meeting transcripts and feedback to improve placement, host selection, and future introductions.

The goal is not passive browsing. The goal is active human placement and interaction.

## Profile First

The profile must be built through active voice conversation with AI. It should not begin as a written form or diary. The AI should learn how the user thinks, talks, jokes, listens, opens up, and responds to others.

The profile should capture signals such as:

- personality traits,
- communication style,
- social energy,
- warmth and openness,
- humor and lightness,
- trust style,
- values,
- interests and taste,
- group comfort,
- one-to-one compatibility.

The user should be able to review and correct the profile, but the app should feel conversational first.

## Circles

Circles are small groups based on personality compatibility. They are the primary product object.

AI is responsible for creating circles, suggesting circles, and explaining why a user fits. People with similar personality traits, communication styles, social energy, and trust patterns should be placed together. People who do not match the circle's trait profile should not be placed into that circle just because they share an interest.

Circle placement should be selective. A circle should feel like the right room, not a broad channel.

## Communities

Communities are based on interests and taste. Examples include jazz, hip hop, books, startups, trekking, design, or cooking.

A user can belong to multiple communities. Communities are broader than circles and can be easier entry points. Community fit is based mostly on liking, curiosity, and participation intent, while circle fit is based mostly on personality and interaction compatibility.

## Meetings

Circle and community meetings should happen inside the app first. In-app meetings allow AI to transcribe the conversation and understand how the group is actually functioning.

The AI should learn:

- who is vocal,
- who is quiet but engaged,
- who includes others,
- who dominates,
- who brings positive energy,
- who breaks tension,
- who makes shy people comfortable,
- whether the group feels warmer after the meeting.

Long term, the AI can coordinate offline meetings such as restaurant or cafe bookings. The product should move from in-app interaction to real-world connection when the group has enough trust and fit.

## Hosts

Hosts should not be selected only because they volunteer or because they have hosted many meetings. The AI should interview volunteers and evaluate their live meeting behavior.

Good host signals:

- happy-go-lucky social energy,
- warmth,
- inclusive conversation,
- ability to break the ice,
- ability to invite quieter people in,
- reliability,
- low dominance,
- comfort with the circle's personality.

A person who hosted many meetings may still be a poor host if they make the room colder or dominate the group. A person with fewer meetings may be a better host if they naturally make people comfortable.

## AI Responsibility

The AI is responsible for encouraging interaction. It should not stop at recommendations.

The AI should:

- create circles,
- suggest circles,
- suggest communities,
- explain fit,
- open safe first conversations,
- identify hosts,
- review host performance,
- track group chemistry,
- suggest future meetings,
- help likeminded people become friends, collaborators, or soulmates when the fit is real.

The product promise is simple: the AI finds the right people and helps the first meaningful interaction happen.
